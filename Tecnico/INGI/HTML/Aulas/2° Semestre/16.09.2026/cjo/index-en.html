Discover the City

Located in the Mantiqueira Mountains, Campos do Jordão is the highest municipality in Brazil. It is famous for its European-style architecture, cold climate, and traditional International Winter Festival.

Top Attractions

Vila Capivari

Tourist center filled with restaurants, chocolate shops, and half-timbered style buildings.

Elephant Hill

Offers an incredible panoramic view of the city, accessible by cable car or car.

Amantikir Park

Themed gardens from various parts of the world with breathtaking landscapes.

Travel Tips

  • Best time: June to August (Winter).
  • Climate: Temperatures can drop below 0°C (32°F) at night.
  • Gastronomy: Try the famous fondues and artisanal chocolates.
