About Jesus Christ

Jesus was born in Bethlehem, Judea, in a humble setting that contrasted with the prophecies announcing him as the Messiah. The son of Mary and raised by Joseph, a carpenter, he spent his youth in Nazareth, where he grew in wisdom. At the age of twelve, he already displayed a profound knowledge of the Scriptures at the Temple in Jerusalem, signaling that his path would be extraordinary. His public ministry began around the age of thirty, after being baptized by John the Baptist. Jesus traveled through cities preaching repentance, love for one's neighbor, and the coming of the Kingdom of God. Through simple parables, he taught complex concepts of ethics and spirituality, while his miracles—such as healing the sick, multiplying food, and walking on water—attracted crowds and confirmed his divine authority to his followers. However, his message challenged the religious and political structures of the time. After three years of preaching, Jesus was betrayed by one of his disciples, Judas Iscariotes, and handed over to the authorities. Sentenced to death by crucifixion by the Roman governor Pontius Pilate, he faced Calvary under humiliation, dying on a Friday at Mount Golgotha to, according to the Scriptures, pay for the sins of humanity. The conclusion of his biblical biography is marked by the supernatural. On the third day after his death, the tomb was found empty, and Jesus appeared resurrected to his disciples. After spending forty days teaching them about the future of the church, he ascended into heaven, leaving the promise that he would return. His short life, of approximately 33 years, became the foundation of Christianity and the primary moral reference for the Western world.

"I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end, the first and the last"

The expression "I am the Alpha and the Omega" is a divine title found in the biblical book of Revelation, referring to Jesus or God as the beginning and the end of all things. It signifies eternity, omnipotence, and that He is the creator and finisher of history.
