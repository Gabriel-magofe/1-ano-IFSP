About the event

The event will take place in Campos do Jordão, with lectures and activities from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The event will have a strong focus on awareness, covering topics from AI to advanced astronomy.

The event aims to promote engagement of women in science and technology, highlighting their contributions and encouraging active participation in research and innovation.

Professional level

The event is aimed at students, professionals, and enthusiasts of science and technology, offering a unique opportunity for learning, networking, and exchanging experiences.

The event will feature renowned professionals from the field, who will share their experiences and knowledge with participants. For example, an excellent professional in the field, postdoctoral researcher Matheus Magalhães Furtado — who has three postdoctoral backgrounds in physics, mathematics, and astronomy — will give two outstanding lectures, providing unique opportunities.

Event instructions

To participate in the event, it is necessary to register in advance through the form available on the registration page. Places are limited, so it is recommended to secure your participation as soon as possible.

During the event, it is important to follow the organization’s guidance, respect the schedule, and maintain a collaborative and respectful learning environment.

Upon arrival, participants must present their ID/CPF for identification, and a badge for access to the event will be issued at that time. However, it should be noted that the event has a small registration fee of R$ 40, which covers meal costs and other activities.

History and purpose:

The event was created to promote gender equality in science and technology by encouraging women's active participation in these fields. The initiative aims to highlight women's contributions to research and innovation, providing a space for learning, networking, and exchanging experiences.

The purpose of the event is to inspire and encourage women to pursue careers in science and technology, offering opportunities for professional and personal development while raising awareness of the importance of diversity and inclusion in these fields.

Event objectives and highlights:

The event's main objectives are to:

  • Promote women's active participation in science and technology;
  • Highlight women's contributions to research and innovation;
  • Offer opportunities for learning, networking, and exchanging experiences;
  • Inspire and encourage women to pursue careers in science and technology;
  • Raise awareness of the importance of diversity and inclusion in these fields;
  • Explain and teach science and technology concepts in an accessible way.
